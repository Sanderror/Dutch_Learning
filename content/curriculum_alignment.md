Source: Sander's own lesson slides (8 lessons), his A1 practice document and two
sets of word cards, read on 14 November 2026. The files themselves are not in
this repository because they contain personal details; this note keeps only
what the course needs.

Exposure is not mastery: these were taught in occasional lessons, not drilled
daily. The course therefore still starts at zero, and the unit test-out lets
her skip what she really knows.

## Consequences for the course

1. **Test-out per unit** is part of the first app version. Sections 1 and 2
   will mostly be review for her.
2. **Reuse her vocabulary.** When a unit needs nouns, verbs or example
   situations, take them from the lists below first, so the app consolidates
   the lessons she has had.
3. **Follow the same conventions:** tú and ustedes; "carro", "celular";
   jouw for tu; "dank je" and "dankjewel"; "doei" and "doeidoei"; mooi and
   knap for bonito/guapa; heel and erg for muy.
4. **Two points where the course is stricter than the lessons were:**
   wonen (to live somewhere) versus leven (to be alive), and hij wil
   (not "hij wilt").
